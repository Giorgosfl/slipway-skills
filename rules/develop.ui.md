# Develop: UI

Rules from develop.md that apply only to apps with a user interface.

- **UI rules.**
  - A screen or panel is as tall as its content, with no large empty band below it.
  - Every layout works in each orientation and window size the platform allows.
  - Text at the largest accessibility size wraps in whole words, without clipping.
  - Every control reads its own name and role to the screen reader.
  - The platform's standard gestures and shortcuts win over custom ones.
  - Controls take the colours of the surface they sit on.
  - Check it: look at every changed screen at its smallest and largest size, and with the screen reader on.
- **Platform-recommended.**
  - "Platform recommended" means the platform's official design guidelines.
  - Where the guidelines are silent, follow how the platform's own apps do it.
  - Example: on iOS, Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, then Apple Books for a reader.
